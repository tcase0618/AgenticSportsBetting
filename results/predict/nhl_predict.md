# NHL game predictor: winner + final score

Generated by `python -m engine.predict.nhl` (5.8 min). Strict walk-forward: every month from 2018 the models are refit on all games dated before that month (2016 onward) and predict the month. Model picked on 2018-2020 log loss; numbers reported for 2021+ (8,052 games) were never used to pick anything.

* Seasons labelled by end year (2021 = 2020-21 COVID season; 2027 = 2026-27 in progress). Final scores include the OT/shootout winner, so there are no ties.
* Closing moneylines are missing for most of 2022-23 and half of 2025-26; those games use the script as the market fallback (has_mkt=0) and are excluded from model-vs-market comparisons.
* Starting goalie (backup / new flags, Kalman goalie effect) is the actual starter, which is normally confirmed before puck drop; treat goalie features as 'confirmed starter' information.
* Puck line: data has the ±1.5 side but no puck-line prices, so break-even uses typical prices (-1.5 at +170, +1.5 at -210).
* Market: closing moneyline de-vigged with `engine.devig.devig(a, b, 'odds_ratio')`. Quotes dropped as broken: missing, |odds|<100, overround outside 0.995-1.12, de-vigged price outside the sport's plausible range or >30 pts away from the script (0 dropped by the last two rules in the predicted seasons). Games without a usable price fall back to the script (flag `has_mkt`=0).

## 5. The formula (plain English)

Win model **lr_small**. Its logistic fit on all games, intercept -0.015; terms per one standard deviation and per raw unit:

| feature   |   coef_per_SD |   coef_raw_unit |     SD |
|:----------|--------------:|----------------:|-------:|
| mkt_logit |        0.3814 |          0.8447 | 0.4516 |
| s_logit   |        0.0867 |          0.1950 | 0.4448 |

In words: logit P(home wins) ≈ +0.845×mkt_logit + +0.195×s_logit + (smaller terms) -0.015. `mkt_logit` = log-odds of the de-vigged closing moneyline, `s_logit` = script log-odds.

Score means (Poisson GLM, log link): log E[home goals] and log E[away goals] = intercept + Σ coef×feature, largest terms:

| feature         |   home_coef_per_SD |   away_coef_per_SD |   home_coef_raw |
|:----------------|-------------------:|-------------------:|----------------:|
| margin          |            -0.0989 |             0.1688 |         -0.1580 |
| core_diff       |             0.0886 |            -0.0916 |          0.2248 |
| sim_logit       |             0.1493 |            -0.0153 |          0.3685 |
| l_mu_away       |             0.0699 |             0.0792 |          0.4872 |
| rls_home        |             0.0349 |            -0.0665 |          0.1895 |
| rls_away        |            -0.0521 |             0.0271 |         -0.2798 |
| mkt_gap         |             0.0539 |            -0.0219 |          0.2832 |
| shots_away      |            -0.0269 |             0.0416 |         -0.0083 |
| goalie_away_eff |            -0.0371 |             0.0298 |         -0.3080 |
| goalie_home_eff |             0.0249 |            -0.0389 |          0.2093 |
| l_mu_home       |            -0.0487 |             0.0094 |         -0.3752 |
| mkt_logit       |            -0.0292 |            -0.0212 |         -0.0646 |

So predicted goals ≈ the script's expected goals pulled toward the closing total (`l_tl`) and the moneyline-implied strength, then the NB grid turns the two means into a full score distribution.

## Does more history help?

Data start 2016, so 'last 6' and 'all' differ only by the 2016 season for 2023. High-confidence tier = pick probability ≥65%.
