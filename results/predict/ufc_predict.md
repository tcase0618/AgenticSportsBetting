# UFC fight predictor: winner + method + round

Generated by `python -m engine.predict.ufc` (2.1 min). Strict walk-forward: every month from 2018 the models are refit on all fights dated before that month (2016 onward) and predict that month's fights. Models picked on 2018-2020; reported 2021+ (2,969 fights with a winner) was never used to pick anything.

* "Final score" of a fight = winner + method (KO/TKO, SUB, DEC) + ending round (a decision ends in the last scheduled round).
* Market: closing moneyline (a_ml/b_ml) de-vigged with `engine.devig.devig(a, b, 'odds_ratio')`; dropped when missing, |odds|<100, overround outside 0.995-1.15 or >45 pts from the script (6 dropped). Moneylines are missing for 15-26% of 2023-24 fights and 74% of 2026 fights; those use the script (has_mkt=0). Method props: the script's power6 de-vig with its quality gate (2025-26 props are mostly broken and are gated out).
* Fighter stats (age, reach, record, streaks, strike/grapple rates) come from data/processed/ufc.csv and are pre-fight (checked: career win counts increment only after the fight). Fighter A is the red corner except in 2026, where most fights are listed alphabetically (corner unknown); every model is trained on mirrored copies of each fight (A<->B) so the only corner effect is the explicit `corner_sign` feature.
