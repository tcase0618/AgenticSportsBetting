## Wind Wall Unders  (NFL)
**Rule:** Bet the UNDER at the closing total in any NFL game played in sustained wind of 15+ mph.
**Why it works:** Wind degrades deep passing and field goals far more than totals adjust for. The edge survives even at the closing number, after the forecast was already public.

## Youth Over Experience  (UFC)
**Rule:** At 155 lbs and below (incl. all women's divisions), bet the moneyline FAVORITE when that fighter is 5+ years younger than the opponent.
**Why it works:** Speed and reaction time decline fastest in the lighter divisions, and the market prices record and name value more heavily than age. Younger favorites win more often than their price implies. Young underdogs show no edge, so this is mispricing at the top of the market, not a general 'youth' effect.
