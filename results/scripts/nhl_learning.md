# NHL script-then-learn: learning report

Generated by `python -m engine.scripts.nhl` (6.3 min). Seasons labelled by end year (2016 = 2015-16; 2027 = the 2026-27 season in progress). Games 2008-2027 run sequentially (2008-2015 warm-up), reported 2016+. Script = goals per side from the shared core (count model) + learned starting-goalie Kalman effect + online RLS correction on pre-game features (back-to-back, backup/new goalie, decayed shots-for/against pace and power-play goals from NHL API boxscores, regular season 2015-16 onward). Win probability from `simulate_scores(count_model=True)` (ties = OT/SO, split 50/50), then online logistic calibration; blend = online logistic of script and de-vigged closing moneyline. Tuning used only 2016-2020.

Data notes: closing moneylines missing for most of 2022-23 (342 of 1400 games) and half of 2025-26; 2026-27 lines from the ESPN feed. Special teams = power-play goals for/against only (PP opportunities are not in the boxscore endpoint).

## Verdict

* Moneyline: the closing market is more accurate than the script by 0.0040 log loss per game on 2021+ (0.6617 vs 0.6577).
* Learned blend vs market: +0.0004 log loss (negative = blend better).
* Totals: script MAE 1.849 vs closing total 1.847.
* Ablation (delta when removed; positive = component helped): no_goalie_rating: ll -0.0007, total MAE -0.001; no_rls(all features): ll +0.0051, total MAE +0.001; core_only: ll +0.0055, total MAE +0.001; no_rest: ll +0.0026, total MAE +0.000; no_goalie_context: ll -0.0000, total MAE -0.000; no_shots_pace: ll +0.0033, total MAE +0.002; no_special_teams: ll +0.0004, total MAE +0.001

## Notes / interpretation
* The script is closest to the market of any MLB/NHL component set: 2021+ log loss 0.6617 vs 0.6577 (gap 0.004), and
  totals essentially tie (MAE 1.849 vs 1.847). It never beats the close; the blend is +0.0004 worse than the market alone
  and flat-betting disagreements loses (-1.8% ROI script, -1.1% blend, 2021+).
* What helped (2021+ ablation, log loss): the RLS feature layer as a whole (+0.0051 when removed), driven by shots-based
  pace from NHL API boxscores (+0.0033) and back-to-back rest (+0.0026); special teams (PP goals) small (+0.0004).
  The goalie Kalman rating (goals allowed vs script) is slightly harmful out of sample (-0.0007) and backup/new-goalie
  flags are neutral: goals-against is too noisy to rate goalies, and the market already prices confirmed starters.
* Totals bias: closing totals sit 0.16 goals below the realised mean (the line is a median, and shootout winners count);
  the script's mean is unbiased (-0.02).
* Player layer couples SOG to scripted team shots and points to scripted team goals. Out-of-sample it beats a league
  mean baseline (SOG MAE 1.06 vs 1.21; points 0.53 vs 0.60), but on the 505 SOG / 405 points real lines it is worse than
  the market (log loss 0.695 vs 0.686; 0.646 vs 0.640). ROI figures on these few hundred lines (best price across books)
  are noise.
