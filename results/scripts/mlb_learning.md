# MLB script-then-learn: learning report

Generated by `python -m engine.scripts.mlb` (8.5 min). Games 2010-2026 run sequentially (2010-2015 warm-up), reported 2016+. Script = runs per side from the shared core (count model) + learned starting-pitcher and park Kalman effects + an online RLS correction on pre-game features. Win probability from `simulate_scores(count_model=True)` (ties = extra innings, 52% home), then an online logistic calibration; blend = online logistic of script and de-vigged closing moneyline. Market total = closing total line. Tuning (grid below) used only 2016-2020.

Data notes: 2010-2025 closing lines from data/processed/mlb.csv; 2026 results from data/live/mlb.csv with closing lines only for Sep 14-Oct 14 2026 (210 games), starters for 2026 taken from Statcast. Statcast pitcher-game data (K, BF, CSW) coverage by season:

## Verdict

* Moneyline: the closing market is more accurate than the script by 0.0038 log loss per game on 2021+ (0.6767 vs 0.6729).
* Learned blend vs market: +0.0004 log loss (negative = blend better).
* Totals: script MAE 3.492 vs closing total 3.419.
* Ablation (delta when removed; positive log-loss delta = component helped): no_sp_rating: ll -0.0000, total MAE +0.001; no_park: ll +0.0000, total MAE +0.015; no_rls(all features): ll +0.0008, total MAE +0.005; core_only: ll +0.0015, total MAE +0.026; no_statcast_sp: ll +0.0008, total MAE +0.005; no_sp_context: ll -0.0004, total MAE +0.000; no_bullpen: ll +0.0000, total MAE -0.001; no_lineup_k: ll +0.0001, total MAE -0.000

## Notes / interpretation
* The market's total line sits ~0.4 runs below the realised mean (a line is a median of a right-skewed count, priced
  with juice), so the script's total bias near 0 is expected; MAE is the fairer comparison and the market still wins
  (3.42 vs 3.49 on 2021+). The biggest relative misses are early-season / new starters and playoffs (script +0.86 runs
  too high in playoff games: aces + leverage bullpens, which a regular-season model does not know).
* What actually helped (2021+ ablation): Statcast starter K%/CSW features (-0.0008 log loss, -0.005 total MAE) and the
  park factor (totals: -0.015 MAE). The run-allowed-based pitcher Kalman rating adds ~nothing once Statcast K/CSW is in
  (it helped on 2016-2020 tuning before Statcast features were learned: 0.6779 -> 0.6764). Bullpen fatigue (relievers'
  pitches previous 3 days), rest/new-starter context and lineup K% are neutral within noise.
* Learned blend weight on the market drifts 0.5-0.85; the blend never beats the closing moneyline (+0.0004 log loss).
  Flat-betting the script's disagreements with the close loses (-1.9% ROI 2021+), as expected from an efficient close.
* K props: the coupled K model is clearly worse than DK/FD lines (log loss 0.708 vs 0.684). It regresses short-outing
  starters (openers, pitch-count limits) up and high-K aces down: at lines of 2.5 the model says 61% over vs 52% realised;
  at 7.5 it says 43% vs 40.5%. Its mean correlates 0.41 with actual Ks vs 0.47 for the market line; most of the gap is
  expected batters faced (corr 0.49 with actual BF), i.e. pitch-count / role information the market has. The online
  blend only ties the market (0.6841 vs 0.6843). No edge.
